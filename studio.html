Common Questions

Do I need my own domain?

If you have one, we'll connect it. If not, I'll set one up (~$25/year) — it stays registered in your name, always.

What are the ongoing costs?

Hosting from around $8/month with the provider of your choice — or nothing if we host on free infrastructure. There's no fee to me after handover.

Can you match my existing branding?

Yes — send logos, colours, photos, and the design follows them. Need a fresh look instead? That's covered too.

Why are you cheaper than agencies?

Low overheads and a tight, focused process. One-page sites don't need a five-person team — just good design and clean code.
