How it works

Your driveway, our salon.

No car stress, no cage time, no waiting around other dogs. Back inside before they know they left.

About

One groomer. One van. Zero cage dryers.

Tide & Tail is a one-person mobile salon based in Browns Bay. Every dog is groomed start-to-finish by the same hands — no hand-offs, no assembly line, no cage dryers ever.

We started in 2021 after ten years in a busy salon, because the dogs who hated shops the most were the ones who did best at home.
